GAMEPLAY
- Portrait only.
- Truck faces down-screen and reverses upward.
- LEFT / RIGHT changes lane only across a safe connected surface.
- GRAB / LAY picks panel stacks or places a missing panel in the work zone.
- Maximum panel inventory: 30.
- Clean panels build score; dirty panels cost score and reduce traction when driven over.
- Grass, mud and water are not playable route surfaces.
- Missing gaps damage vehicle and traction.
- Occupied work zones require waiting.
- OHL/GS6 sections use a visible LOCK OFF game control before the required panel operation.
- Taco Breaks are checkpoints with awareness reminders.

IMPORTANT
This is an arcade / safety-awareness game. Vehicle speed, timing, distances, crane movements and controls are stylised and must not be recreated on site. Real work follows the current approved job pack, method statement, DRA, lift plan, site controls and Responsible Person instructions.
